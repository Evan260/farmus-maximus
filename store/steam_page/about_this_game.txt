[h2]Your uncle has left you his farm. It has seen better days.[/h2]

Farmus Maximus is a cozy, semi-idle farming game on a Roman country estate in the hills. Plant fields of lavender and wheat, bring ruined workshops back to life, and restore the old farmhouse stage by stage into the grandest villa in the valley. Play a little or a lot: the farm keeps growing either way.

[h2]From ruin to grand estate[/h2]
The farmhouse starts as a crumbling ruin. Restore it through seven stages, from a simple restored farmhouse to a villa with a portico, a peristyle garden, a bathhouse wing and finally a grand estate. Every step changes what you see.

[h2]Grow, make and sell[/h2]
[list]
[*]Grow four kinds of lavender, including a rare white one, along with wheat, olives, herbs and grapes.
[*]Restore ten workshops and a household shrine: dry lavender, grind flour at the mill, bake bread, keep bees, press olive oil and wine, age wine in the cellar, make perfumed oils, keep hens and goats in the barn and make cheese in the dairy.
[*]Goods come in three qualities, and the finest fetch the best prices.
[*]Send your cart to the market town, fill orders from the regulars on the notice board, and time your sales around the Roman festivals of Floralia, Vinalia, Cerealia and Saturnalia.
[/list]

[h2]Helpers keep it running[/h2]
Hire seven helpers, each with a name and something to say: a farmhand, a miller, a beekeeper, a presser, a perfumer, a stable hand and a trader. Watch them pick the harvest, carry flour to the bakery and load the cart, and walk home through their own front doors at nightfall. Close the game and the estate carries on for up to twelve hours while you are away.

[h2]Made to sit beside your work[/h2]
Shrink Farmus Maximus to a strip along the bottom of your screen and let the farm run while you work, study or browse. A glance tells you what is ready.

[h2]Little joys[/h2]
[list]
[*]A dog and a cat to choose, name and pet, in several breeds. The dog sometimes digs up old coins, truffles or a rare seed.
[*]Furnish the farmhouse and spend evenings by the fire.
[*]Make offerings at the household shrine for the blessings of Ceres, Bacchus, Minerva, Pomona, Mercury and the Lares.
[*]Seasons, rain and mist, fireflies over the lavender at night, the stork on the roof, a rare hoopoe, a passing fox, and on a few lucky nights the northern lights.
[*]A journal of everything you discover, 46 Steam achievements and three farms to keep.
[/list]

Nothing dies, nothing spoils and there are no timers to dread. Just a quiet corner of Rome to bring back to life.
