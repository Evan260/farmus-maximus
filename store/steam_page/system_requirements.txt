WINDOWS

Minimum
OS: Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
Processor: any 64-bit dual-core processor from the last ten years
Memory: 2 GB RAM
Graphics: any graphics card or integrated graphics supporting OpenGL 3.3
Storage: 200 MB available space

Recommended
Memory: 4 GB RAM
Additional notes: Runs happily in the background while you work.


MACOS

Minimum
OS: macOS 13 Ventura or later on Apple silicon, macOS 11 Big Sur or later on Intel
Processor: Apple silicon or a 64-bit Intel processor
Memory: 2 GB RAM
Graphics: any Mac that runs these versions of macOS
Storage: 200 MB available space

Recommended
Memory: 4 GB RAM
Additional notes: Runs happily in the background while you work.


LINUX / STEAMOS

Minimum
OS: a 64-bit distribution with glibc 2.28 or later (Ubuntu 20.04, Debian 10, Fedora 29 or newer), or SteamOS
Processor: any 64-bit dual-core processor from the last ten years
Memory: 2 GB RAM
Graphics: any graphics card or integrated graphics supporting OpenGL 3.3
Storage: 200 MB available space

Recommended
Memory: 4 GB RAM
Additional notes: Runs happily in the background while you work.
