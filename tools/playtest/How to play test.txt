FARMUS MAXIMUS - PLAY TEST

Thanks for trying the game! It is still being made, so expect rough edges.


STARTING THE GAME

1. Unzip this folder anywhere, for example on your Desktop.
2. Double-click "Farmus Maximus.exe".
3. Windows may show a blue box saying "Windows protected your PC". That is
   because the game is new and not yet signed. Click "More info", then
   "Run anyway".

Nothing needs installing. The game saves by itself every minute and when
you close it, and carries on growing while it is closed.


CONTROLS

Mouse          Click plants, buildings, animals and people
A / D          Scroll along the estate (or drag with the mouse)
Q / E          Jump to the previous / next part of the estate
1 - 9          Pick a tool or seed from the bar at the bottom
J              Farmer's journal
M              Ride to the market town
Esc            Close a window
F10            Shrink the game to a strip along the edge of your desktop
               (press again to go back)

Keys can be changed in Settings > Keys.


WHAT TO TELL ME

Anything at all is useful, especially:

- Anything confusing: when you did not know what to do next, or what
  something meant.
- Anything that felt slow, boring or too fast.
- Anything that looked wrong or broken. A screenshot helps a lot:
  press Windows key + Shift + S, drag over the problem, then paste it
  into a message.
- What you liked.
- Roughly how long you had played when you noticed each thing.

If the game ever closes by itself or will not start, tell me what you were
doing just before.


YOUR SAVE

Your farm is saved in:
  %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Farmus Maximus\
(paste that into the address bar of File Explorer to open it)

If I ask for your save, zip up that folder and send it to me.
